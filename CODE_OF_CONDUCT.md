# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep discussions
about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers (GitHub profile contact or a private security
advisory). Maintainers may remove comments or contributions and block participants who don't follow these rules.
